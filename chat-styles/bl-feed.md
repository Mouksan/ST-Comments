### CRITICAL RULES FOR REALISM (MUST FOLLOW):
- **LANGUAGE:** Russian only. Keep the characters' names exactly as the story writes them.
- **BROKEN PUNCTUATION:** lowercase, no final periods, ))))), ???, keysmash (аааааа, ахвхахвх, ЫЫЫ), typos, «пж», «чел», «всмысле», «я не дышу».
- **INCOMPLETE THOUGHTS:** quote a line and react, or drop a single word. Nobody explains the joke.
- **BL SPACE SLANG:** weave in naturally where it fits — мои мальчики, они женаты (я сказала), ОТП, шипперю, канон/хэдканон, топ/боттом, сэмэ/укэ, гун/шоу, свитч, гринфлаг/редфлаг, собственник, стекло, флафф, ангст, слоуберн, вкуснятина, горячо, пожар, ДЫШИМ, помянем, прода когда, автор я вас люблю/ненавижу, переводчикам низкий поклон.
- **THEY ARE HERE FOR BEAUTIFUL MEN:** squealing over looks is half the feed — faces, hands, veins, shoulders, waists, height difference, scars, outfits, the way one of them LOOKS at the other. Someone always demands visuals («ХОЧУ ВИЗУАЛ», «художники ау», «где арты»).
- **NO BRAKES:** this is an 18+ BL community. When the chapter is hot, commenters thirst openly and crudely — bodies, positions, who tops, what they would do — no euphemisms, no pearl-clutching. Not every comment is horny: feelings, plot and jokes still carry most of the feed.
- **BABIES & EGGS:** when the chapter hints at pregnancy, a clutch or spawn, the feed loses its mind and invents its OWN nicknames for the little ones from this story's details — species, setting, the parents' names and quirks: live babies, eggs, roe, whatever this universe does. Tone seeds only, never reuse them verbatim: «пузожитель», «булочка в духовке», «кронпринц на подходе», «яйцо с характером папаши». Every commenter coins a different one.
- **THIS STORY FIRST:** commenters react to THIS story and its heroes. A comparison to some other title is allowed only when a joke truly needs it — rare, never a ritual.
- **ORGANIC REACTION COUNTS:** ragged, uneven numbers (7, 23, 148, 1.3K) — most messages get a few or none, only one or two blow up. Never a neat row of round hundreds.
